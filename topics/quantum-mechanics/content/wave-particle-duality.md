## The idea

For a century, physicists argued: is light a wave or a stream of particles? Diffraction and interference proved it was a wave. Then the photoelectric effect proved it arrives in discrete particle-like packets. Both camps were right. Light is a single kind of thing that reveals *wave-like* properties in some experiments and *particle-like* properties in others. Ask it a wave question, get a wave answer; ask a particle question, get a particle answer. The object hasn't changed — your question has.

## Why it matters

Duality shatters the comfortable idea that everything is either a tiny billiard ball or a ripple. It says the fundamental constituents of the universe are something else entirely, for which "wave" and "particle" are just two shadows cast on the wall. Crucially, this isn't limited to light: soon de Broglie will argue that electrons, atoms, even molecules carry a wave nature too. Duality is the doorway from classical intuition into genuinely quantum thinking, and everything downstream — interference, uncertainty, the wavefunction — depends on taking it seriously.

## The math (light)

The two faces are bridged by a single equation linking a particle property (momentum) to a wave property (wavelength):

**p = h / λ**

- **p** — momentum, a particle-like quantity
- **λ** (lambda) — wavelength, a wave-like quantity
- **h** — Planck's constant, the tiny conversion factor between the two worlds

That one relation says momentum and wavelength are two descriptions of the same underlying thing. Big momentum means short wavelength, and vice versa.

## See it

Consider a single photon fired at a detector screen. It lands as one sharp dot — unmistakably a particle, one click. But fire millions of single photons through a pair of slits, one at a time, and the pattern of dots slowly builds up into bright and dark bands — an interference pattern only a wave can make. Same photons, same setup: each individual arrival is a particle, yet the collective statistics are dictated by a wave. Neither picture alone is the whole truth.
